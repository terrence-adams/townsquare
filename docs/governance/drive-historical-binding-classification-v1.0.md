# Historical Drive binding classification v1.0

**document_id:** `TS-DRIVE-HISTORICAL-CLASSIFICATION-20261006`
**status:** HISTORICAL CLASSIFICATION — NOT AUTHORITY — NOT A BINDING

Google Drive is permanently classified as a historical external input and, where an older implementation mapped governance to it, as historical binding evidence. It is not an authority source, a required review substrate, a source of current governance, or a prerequisite for adoption, deployment, or conformance.

Historical material may be cited for provenance using its known identity/hash/date and must be labelled `HISTORICAL_EXTERNAL_INPUT` or `HISTORICAL_BINDING_EVIDENCE`. Missing access, a missing mirror, or an unavailable historical item becomes `UNKNOWN`; it never silently grants authority or blocks an otherwise complete local review. Any future storage implementation requires its own separately accepted binding and does not revive Drive authority.
