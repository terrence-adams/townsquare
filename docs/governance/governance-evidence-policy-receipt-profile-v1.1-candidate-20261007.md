# Governance Evidence and Policy Receipt Profile v1.1 — candidate

**document_id:** `TS-GOV-EVIDENCE-RECEIPT-1.1-CANDIDATE-20261007`
**status:** CANDIDATE — NOT ADOPTED

Two vocabularies are deliberately separate. **Artifact readiness** is `CANDIDATE`, `ADOPTED`, `WITHDRAWN`, or `SUPERSEDED`; it says whether an artifact is proposed or selected. **Evidence observation** is `REPORTED`, `VERIFIED`, `STALE`, `REVOKED`, or `UNKNOWN`; it says what an observer established about a cited artifact/action. Neither vocabulary is a Request lifecycle state, and neither alone grants authority.

A receipt records the exact governance and binding dependency set, each dependency's adoption reference and observation state, actor, represented authority, action/effect, current revision, criteria, authority-resolution result, and immutable audit reference. It proves retrieval/evaluation only, never comprehension, intent, agreement, or future obedience.
